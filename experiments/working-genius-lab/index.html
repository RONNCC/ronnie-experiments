How the experiment scores your answers

Each type has four questions. Its score is their arithmetic mean. Questions are interleaved so the type is not shown while answering. No question is reverse scored. When no tie crosses a category boundary, the top two are labeled experimental genius, the middle two competency, and the bottom two frustration.

These labels are relative within your answers: lowest does not necessarily mean draining, and highest does not necessarily mean strongly energizing. A tie crossing a category boundary leaves the affected category unresolved. No arbitrary tie-breaker picks a winner. All scores remain visible. Uniform ratings cannot support a meaningful ranking.

This is not the official scoring method. No reliability, validity, population norms or equivalence to the official test has been established. Do not use it to make hiring or performance decisions.

A plain-language glossary

Six types describe activities. Three categories describe a person's relationship to those activities.

Keep the model in perspective.

Being drained by an activity does not make you bad at it. Getting energy from an activity does not prove you do it well. Roles, rest, team support and practice still matter. Try noticing your energy over several actual projects before changing how you work.
