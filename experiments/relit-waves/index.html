Cultural periodization

Everybody kept counting to three.

Coffee has a third wave. So do sociolinguistics, ska, chocolate, therapy, interface design, AI, democracy, and feminism. The phrase spread because it carries a claim: something was flattened, and a later generation went back for it.

A relit wave is a domain reworked by a new cohort in reaction to what the previous era standardized or left out, then numbered to claim the lineage.

Who started counting

Each label carries a status. Some are institutional terms with a citable first use, such as Huntington's third wave of democratization in 1991 or Bødker's third wave HCI in 2006. Others are borrowed craft analogies, like bean to bar chocolate echoing coffee, or fan periodizations, like the disputed fifth wave of emo. Where the numbering breaks down, the card says so rather than smoothing it over.

05

When the wave metaphor lies

Ocean waves imply a single crest, then dead calm. Nancy Hewitt's 2010 collection No Permanent Waves points out what that hides: everyone who kept working in the troughs. Her alternative is radio, where several frequencies broadcast at once.
