The Relit Wave

Stumble onto third-wave coffee and a strange question pops up: wait—food has numbered waves? Once you look, the exact same wave pattern shows up in sociolinguistics, ska & emo music, bean-to-bar chocolate, psychotherapy, HCI, AI, and civic history.

A "relit wave" happens when a generation inherits something that feels settled—a grocery tin of coffee, a census map of accents, a workplace desktop—and relights the underlying craft, agency, or politics that standardization flattened out.

Fun genealogy fact: When coffee roaster Trish Rothgeb popularized "Third-Wave Coffee" in 2002–2003, she explicitly borrowed the numbering from third-wave feminism—which traced back to a 1968 New York Times headline.

How to read this guide: Some "waves" are canonical scholarly or industry terms (feminism, coffee, Eckert's sociolinguistics, 2 Tone/3rd-wave ska, Huntington's democratization, Hayes's CBT, Bødker's HCI, DARPA's AI). Others are borrowed craft analogies or fan periodizations (bean-to-bar chocolate, 4th/5th-wave emo, 4th-wave coffee). Every card below labels its exact status—and where the wave metaphor breaks down.

01 / ANATOMY OF A RELIT WAVE

Why do movements come in threes (and retroactive ones)?

Nobody opening a Folgers tin in 1952 or playing Kingston ska in 1962 announced, "Welcome to the First Wave." Wave numbers are retroactive: a movement names itself a Second or Third Wave to claim a lineage while rebelling against the blind spots of the status quo.

PHASE 01 · FOUNDATION

Breakthrough & Scale

Solves a hard foundational problem: putting coffee in every home, winning the ballot, correlating speech with class, or measuring human-machine errors.

PHASE 02 · INSTITUTION

Ritual, Identity & Spread

Turns a commodity or method into a self-conscious culture: espresso "third places," local ethnographies, 2 Tone records, cognitive restructuring, or federal statutes.

PHASE 03 · RELIGHTING

Granularity & Agency

Pushes back against Wave 2's standardization: single-farm light roasts, speaker persona, intersectionality, mindfulness context, or DIY crossover.

PHASE 04 · FRACTURE / LEAPFROG

Science, Synthesis, or Revival

Once "Third Wave" goes mainstream, Wave 4 either gets hyper-technical (refractometers), synthesizes prior waves, or skips Wave 3 to revive Wave 2 (emo revival).

05 / WHEN THE WAVE METAPHOR LIES

Ocean Waves vs. Nancy Hewitt's Radio Frequencies

Why do historians warn against taking "waves" too literally? Ocean waves suggest one giant wall of water followed by dead calm—hiding everyone who kept working in the "troughs" and pretending Folgers or 1st-wave exposure therapy disappeared. Historian Nancy A. Hewitt (No Permanent Waves, 2010) suggested switching the physics from ocean swells to radio waves: simultaneous broadcasts at different frequencies.
