LAB 04 / SYNTHETIC ACCOUNTING, NOT AN AI BENCHMARK

More autonomy is not more evidence.

Eight fixed cases, four seeded design bugs. Per-case rounds, a global occupied-license-hour budget, fixed case order, and required evidence determine supported outcomes. No LLM or simulator runs. No invented performance claims about Bronco. Workers alter an idealized list-schedule time only; assume enough tokens for those workers.

Disclosed cost model and model answer

Every completed round costs a synthetic $0.04 of model calls and two minutes of inference/tool overhead, plus 60 × per-round license-hours of execution time. These are author-selected accounting constants, not provider prices. A round cannot start if it would exceed the remaining global license budget. Partial rounds are not charged. Infrastructure/spec-only cases use zero simulation-license hours, but still consume calls and overhead. Completed case tasks are assigned to the earliest-free worker in fixed case order. No queue, dependencies, compilation, or scheduling overhead is modeled.

Review is six minutes per supported bug, three per supported infrastructure case, two for clean, four for ambiguity, and eight for each unresolved case. These are synthetic review assumptions. False positives are zero by this toy policy's construction, not measured agent accuracy. Missing evidence forces abstention. No confidence interval or real-world success probability follows from this exercise.

Try: max rounds 4, budget 4, both evidence tools on: all eight outcomes are supported, including four bugs and four valid non-bug outcomes. Disable the checker: bug reproductions no longer qualify even at a larger budget. Increase workers: ideal time can improve, but bug yield and charged cost do not. Real evaluations must additionally measure false positives, blind holdout performance, patch quality, and actual engineer time.
