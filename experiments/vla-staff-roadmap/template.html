01 — TRAINING MEMORY▤

Will the optimizer fit?

Idealized BF16 AdamW: 16 bytes per parameter before activations. DDP replicates static state; ideal FSDP shards it. Decimal GB, not GiB. Chapter 9 →

02 — EVALUATION UNCERTAINTY±

What does 84% success mean?

A 95% Wilson score interval for independent Bernoulli trials. It does not capture correlated initial states, changing hardware, or distribution shift. Chapter 11 →

Use whole rollout counts with 0 ≤ successes ≤ trials; percentages must stay within their field limits.

Never compare two checkpoints by point estimates alone. Use the same pre-registered task matrix, report paired initial conditions where possible, and inspect failures. The planning count is only a normal-approximation starting point.

03 — DATA MIXTURE TEMPERATUREα

Who gets the next training sample?

Temperature sampling uses pᵢ ∝ nᵢ^α. At α=1 you sample naturally; at α=0 every dataset has equal probability. Intermediate values rebalance without making tiny datasets disappear. Chapter 7 →
