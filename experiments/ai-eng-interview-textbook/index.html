THE FOUR-PART ANSWER

Choice → reason → trade-off → proof

  1. Here’s how I would approach it — choose a concrete design.
  2. Here’s why — connect it to requirements and evidence.
  3. Here’s what could go wrong — expose failure modes and costs.
  4. Here’s how I would test it — define observable checks and success criteria.

BEFORE YOU PRACTICE

Pick one resume project. Go deep.

Choose the project you can explain from first requirement to measured outcome. Be ready to draw the data flow, name the baseline, defend one rejected alternative, describe a real failure mode, and say what you personally built. Keep claims factual; do not invent impact or disclose confidential details.
