01

The big idea

“Legal AI” is not one architecture. It is a stack: permissioned data + document understanding + evidence retrieval + a model/agent layer + verification + a lawyer’s approval. Different products put their moat in different layers.

05 · INTERVIEW STUDIO

How to answer: “Design legal AI contract review.”

Don’t begin with “use an LLM.” Begin with the legal work product, evidentiary standard, and action boundary.

  1. 01
    Frame the operating context.

    Who relies on it, what can it change, which sources are authoritative, and what confidentiality wall applies?

  2. 02
    Specify the evidence contract.

    Every material finding gets a source span; every cited authority is resolved by metadata, not model free-text.

  3. 03
    Split deterministic from generative work.

    OCR, parsing, entity normalization, exact dates, permissions, and policy checks are services — not prompts.

  4. 04
    Constrain the model and agent.

    Retrieve only permitted context, use schemas/tool calls, set budgets, and make draft / send / file distinct authorization levels.

  5. 05
    Prove it in evals.

    Measure extraction, retrieval, groundedness, calibration, workflow success, and harmful-error rate on a frozen matter-safe set.
