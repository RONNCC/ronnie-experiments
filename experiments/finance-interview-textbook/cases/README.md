# Capstone CSV conventions

Every capstone has a human-readable input CSV under `inputs/` and a derived output CSV under `outputs/`. Inputs are invented teaching assumptions, not market observations or recommendations. The output files are reproducibly generated from the input files by running `node experiments/finance-interview-textbook/scripts/build-capstone-outputs.mjs`; automated tests parse the input fixtures, recalculate results, and compare every output metric.

## Case-specific modeling boundaries

- **Investment banking DCF:** values are in fictional currency units in millions except shares and per-share value. FCFF = EBIT × (1 − tax) + D&A − capex − increase in operating NWC. FCFF is discounted at WACC. The terminal state grows NOPAT at `g` and reinvests `g / terminal ROIC` of terminal NOPAT; perpetual growth is not free. EV-to-equity subtracts debt, preferred and minority claims and adds stated cash. The sensitivity matrix changes WACC and growth together; no claim is made that either assumption is observable precisely.
- **Private-equity LBO:** source/use items are fictional millions. Purchase equity = purchase EV minus old debt (zero acquired cash assumed). Uses include purchase equity plus repayment of that old debt plus fees and minimum cash. Management rollover and sponsor equity participate pro rata in exit equity at the same entry price, with no preferences or incentive dilution. Interim distributions are unsupported because the IRR formula assumes one exit cash flow. Interest is charged on beginning-of-year debt; after cash taxes, capex and working-capital investment, mandatory amortization is paid and 75% of positive remaining cash is swept. There is no revolver, PIK, incremental borrowing or intra-year timing. The transaction funds minimum cash at close; cash retained after the modeled sweep accumulates and is added to equity proceeds at exit. The model fails rather than silently funding mandatory amortization if operating cash is inadequate. Exit equity is multiple × year-five EBITDA less ending debt plus ending cash, less exit fees (with no interim distributions). The downside changes only the exit multiple, so it is a deliberately isolated stress, not a full downside forecast.
- **Private-credit case:** the covenant EBITDA and collateral recovery fraction are expressly assumed. Available liquidity is cash plus committed undrawn revolver less minimum liquidity. The simplified recovery waterfall applies recovery proceeds to the senior secured claim, then other claims; real priority, liens, guarantees, bankruptcy costs, collateral enforceability and intercreditor documents control.
- **Hedge-fund case:** equal risk dollars are allocated to long and short shares based on entry-to-stop distance. Gross/net limits are illustrative and checked against the fictional portfolio equity. Stress marks both legs at the stated stress prices. A stop price is not guaranteed execution; gap, borrow recall, short squeezes, liquidity, factor exposures and correlations can cause losses beyond budget.
- **Markets case:** the bond calculation assumes an option-free annual-coupon bond with annual compounding. Duration/convexity are local yield sensitivities; the move is parallel and omits spread, curve and liquidity change. The European call uses Black–Scholes with zero dividends, continuously compounded risk-free rate, constant volatility, and no transaction cost. The Greek P&L is a local delta-gamma-vega approximation, not a full repricing or hedge guarantee.
- **Equity-research case:** the EPS revision, target multiple, dividend yield and rating thresholds are fictional. The Positive/Neutral/Negative rule is only the case's invented rubric; there is no universal threshold. It omits uncertainty ranges, valuation cross-checks, conflicts disclosures and full financial-statement drivers, which a real memo would need.
- **Wealth-management case:** fictional dollars and annual end-of-year deposits. The projection assumes constant nominal returns, inflation and contributions, and separately applies a hypothetical withdrawal tax rate to the entire ending balance; actual account basis/tax treatment differs. The withdrawal-rate heuristic and goals are illustrative, not advice or a guarantee. Tax and legal rules vary by jurisdiction and facts; no current thresholds are asserted.

- **M&A accretion/dilution:** fictional millions except per-share values. Full-year close and full run-rate synergies; consideration is target equity value. Tax deductions on amortization, financing and integration costs are explicit simplifying assumptions. No target debt refinancing, fees, NOL constraints or deferred taxes. Recurring and first-year EPS shown separately. Accretion is not NPV.
