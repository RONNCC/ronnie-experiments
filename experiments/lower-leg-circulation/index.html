04 / HOW IT WORKS

Valve & pump mechanism

PUMP ACTIVE
AMuscle contraction — squeeze

Calf contracts and squeezes the deep vein — the valve flaps open and blood shoots toward the heart.

BMuscle relaxation — refill

As the muscle relaxes, the valve snaps shut — blood can't fall back, and the vein refills from below.
