Healthy valves guide blood upward and snap shut against backflow. A clot can scar the leaflets and stretch the vein wall, leaving a leaky valve with reflux — one more reason the full ~3-month healing window (below) matters.

04 / HOW IT WORKS

Valve & pump mechanism

AMuscle contraction — squeeze

During the modeled calf squeeze, a proximal gate opens while a distal gate closes to limit backflow.

BMuscle relaxation — refill

As the calf relaxes, the proximal gate closes; a distal gate can open to let the vein refill from below.

06 / RECOVERY

Why 3 months matters

Anticoagulants hold the line while your body clears the clot

PROTECTED · ON TREATMENT

Anticoagulants don’t dissolve the clot. They stop it from growing while your body’s own fibrinolytic system breaks it down and the vein recanalizes (reopens). That cleanup takes roughly 3 months — stopping early leaves a half-dissolved clot that can regrow.

Diagnosis~3 months
